# Point of Sale (POS) Guide — Fratelanza ERP / PharmaPOS v2

**Menu:** Point of Sale | **Version:** 2.0 | **Languages:** Arabic / English

---

## Overview

The POS is the daily cashier screen. It supports barcode scanning, line and invoice discounts, multiple payment methods, delivery and digital sales, held carts, clinic prescriptions, thermal receipts, and automatic branch stock deduction.

**Offline:** Online-only today; offline POS with sync is on the roadmap.

---

## Before selling — open a shift

Sales are blocked until a **cash drawer shift** is open:

1. Go to **Cash Drawer / Shifts**
2. **Open shift** and enter opening cash
3. Return to POS

Close the shift at end of day for X/Z reports.

---

## Adding products

- **Barcode scanner:** focus the search field and scan (supports internal codes, branch suffixes, wrapper chars, GTIN)
- **Name search:** type and pick from the dropdown
- **Pack / sub-unit:** toggle box vs tablet/strip when pack size is configured

---

## Cart actions

- Quantity +/-, remove line, clear cart
- **Line discount** — amount (EGP) or percent
- **Invoice discount** — sidebar, amount or percent

---

## Seller & customer

- **Seller** required — select, “Me”, or scan seller card
- **Customer** optional; **name + phone required** for sales over **100 EGP**

---

## Hold / recall

**Suspend** saves the cart locally; **Held** recalls it later.

---

## Checkout & payment

**Sale types:** Cash (in-store) | Delivery | Digital (Talabat, Vezeeta, etc.)

**Payment methods:** Cash | Visa/card | Cash + card | InstaPay | Vodafone Cash | On account (partial pay now allowed)

**Delivery:** customer details, address, fee, driver → appears in Deliveries module.

After payment → **Receipt** → **Print** (configure paper 80mm in Settings → Pharmacy).

---

## After sale

- **Refund receipt** link from POS header
- **Sales history** for all invoices
- **Returns** for partial/full refunds

---

## Tips

1. Keep focus in the scan/search field  
2. Open shift at start of day  
3. Use hold instead of clearing the cart  
4. Second POS window = independent cart on same PC
